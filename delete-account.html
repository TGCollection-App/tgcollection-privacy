Delete Your TGCollection Account

TGCollection allows users to request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

Open TGCollection, sign in to your account, go to Profile → Delete Account, and confirm the deletion.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team.

Send an email from the address associated with your TGCollection account, if possible. Include the subject Account Deletion Request.

Request Account Deletion

Contact: tgcollection.app@outlook.com

We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request to protect your account from unauthorized deletion.

What data is deleted?

Account deletion is intended to remove your TGCollection account, profile, saved collection, favorites, registered devices, notifications, price alert states and advertising reward records associated with your account.

Shared card catalog information, expansion data and general card prices are not tied to your individual account and are not deleted.

Data retention

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate legal obligations. Any such retention will be limited to the necessary purpose and period.
